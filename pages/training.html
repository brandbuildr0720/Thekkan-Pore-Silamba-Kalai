Training & Programs

For Everyone

Who Can Join?

Thekkan Pore Silamba Kalai welcome all students between the age of 7 and 20, from any school, any background, and any walk of life. Silambam is not just a sport — it is a discipline, a lifestyle, and a path. We are not focused on money. We are focused on building a generation that is drug-free, disciplined, physically strong, and mentally sharp. Our students earn free sports quota opportunities, develop real talent, and carry the ancient warrior tradition of Tamil Nadu forward with pride. If you have the heart to learn, we have the place for you.
