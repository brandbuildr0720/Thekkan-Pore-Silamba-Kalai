Our Origin

What is Silambam?

Silambam — The Ancient Staff Art

Silambam is one of the oldest martial arts in the world, originating from the ancient Tamil civilization of South India. Practiced for over 5,000 years, it is a weapon-based art form where the bamboo staff (known as the silambam) is wielded with extraordinary speed, grace, and precision.

Through the Ages

Historical Timeline

BC

Ancient Sangam Era

Silambam is mentioned in ancient Sangam literature as a noble martial discipline practiced by Tamil warriors and kings. References to "Silambam Attam" appear in texts dating back over 5,000 years.

CE

Medieval Period

The art flourished under the Chola, Pandya, and Chera kingdoms. Warrior clans were trained from childhood in silambam as a primary combat skill. The technique became an integral part of Tamil military tradition.

17C

Guerrilla Warfare Era

Tamil warriors used silambam extensively during resistance movements. The art was carried secretly even under colonial suppression, passed from guru to student in hidden gurukuls across Tamil Nadu.

19C

Colonial Suppression & Revival

British colonial rulers banned public practice of martial arts. Despite this, dedicated masters preserved the art in secret. Post-independence, silambam began its revival as a cultural heritage form.

NOW

Today, our school carries forward this unbroken lineage. With 3 branches and dedicated masters, we ensure that the ancient warrior tradition of Thekkan Pore style continues for generations to come.

Our School

What is Thekkan Pore?

THEKKAN PORE SILAMBAM – A HISTORICAL REPORT

Silambam is one of the oldest and most respected traditional martial arts of Tamil Nadu, representing the courage, discipline, and cultural heritage of the Tamil people. Thekkan Pore Silambam stands as a dedicated institution committed to preserving this ancient art through training and social service, positively impacting countless students and the broader community.

Origin and Foundation

Founded by Guru Sahul Ameed, the organization was built on a vision to preserve Silambam for future generations. Under his guidance, Master Thamim Ansari received extensive training and took on the responsibility of teaching and expanding the organization's reach.

During the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020, classes were launched at Otteri Church Ground — conducted entirely free of charge, ensuring every student could access self-defense training regardless of financial hardship.

Growth and Development

As student numbers grew, a minimal fee structure was introduced — supported by parents and the community — keeping classes affordable and accessible for all backgrounds. In 2023, the organization reached a major milestone by registering under the Chengai Tamilan All Sports Association and registered with Tamil Nadu societies, opening doors to official competitions and sporting events.

Student Achievements

Students have excelled at District, Zone, and State-level competitions, earning medals, trophies, and certificates. Beyond the sport, these achievements have opened doors to higher education for many students — especially those from economically disadvantaged backgrounds — with several now pursuing college studies through their sporting accomplishments.

Present Status

From a single ground training session, Thekkan Pore Silambam has grown into 3 successful branches across Chennai, continuing to instill discipline, physical fitness, self-confidence, leadership, and respect in every student.

Conclusion

Thekkan Pore Silambam is more than a martial arts school — it is a social movement empowering youth and preserving Tamil cultural heritage. With its growing impact on student welfare, character development, and community service, the organization proudly carries the legacy of Silambam forward to future generations.

Key Techniques

  • 🥢 STANCE — various stance for various movements
  • 🥢 SINGLE STICK —single stick used for defensive & offensive technique
  • 🥢 DOUBLE STICK — same like single with a double protection & also for improve style
  • 🥢 MAAN KOMBU — fighting technique with maan kombu / deer antler
  • 🥢 VEL KOMBU — spear based weapon used to protect and attack , it improve our foot work,skill,speed,coordination
  • 🥢 CHEDI KUCHI — its a short-stick weapon used in short range combat, impore reflex & control
  • 🥢 SURUL VAAL — flexible sword with several blades, requries high skill,prrecision & control
  • 🥢 VAAL VEECHU — traditional sword used in battle, develop precision, agility & combat techniques
  • 🥢 1 STEP FIGHT — pre-arranged combat drill where one attack and one defense are performed in a single sequence.
  • 🥢 WEAPON SNATCHING — self-defense technique used to disarm an opponent and gain control of their weapon.
